Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung · Last updated 9 November 2026

In short: Future Cash doesn’t collect your data. Your plan stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no accounts and no ads, and the app has no analytics.

Who is responsible

Brandon Oxendine, Emser Straße 115, 12051 Berlin, Germany. Email: brandon.oxendine@gmail.com

The app

Everything you enter (your balance, bills, income, purchases and trips) is stored on your device. If you’re signed in to iCloud, it’s synced between your devices through your private iCloud database, which Apple runs on your behalf. We can’t see or access it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own. Reminders are scheduled on your device and you choose which ones you get; nothing about them leaves the device. Backup files are only created when you export one, and are saved wherever you choose.

To delete your data, use Erase All Data in the app’s settings, or delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage).

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver the pages and keep them secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser type in its server logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The site sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and loads its fonts from this site rather than from third parties.

To know how many people visit and which version of the page works better, the site counts visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly statistics service. For each visit, GoatCounter receives the page address, the site you came from, your browser and screen size, and your approximate country, worked out from your IP address. Your IP address is not stored, and no profile of you is built: GoatCounter only keeps a short-lived anonymous hash so the same visit isn’t counted twice. Taps on the download button are counted the same way. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GoatCounter’s privacy policy.
