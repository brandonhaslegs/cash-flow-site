Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung · Last updated 21 October 2026

In short: Future Cash doesn’t collect your data. Your plan stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

The app

Everything you enter (your balance, bills, income, expenses and trips) is stored on your device. If you’re signed in to iCloud, it’s synced between your devices through your private iCloud database, which Apple runs on your behalf. We can’t see or access it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own. Backup files are only created when you export one, and are saved wherever you choose.

To delete your data, use Erase All Data in the app’s settings on Mac, or delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage).
