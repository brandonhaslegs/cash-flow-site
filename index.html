Questions

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Future Cash never connects to your bank and never sees your accounts or transactions. You type in your balance yourself, and update it whenever you want the latest figures. Your bills and paydays repeat on their own, so a quick balance update is usually all it takes.

How do I keep it up to date?

Open the app, tap your balance and type what your bank shows. Bills and paydays drop off the forecast on their own once their day has passed, so there’s nothing to delete. If one goes out early or arrives late, swipe it to mark it paid or received.

What does “Safe to spend” mean?

It’s the most you could spend today without your balance ever dropping below zero over the next year, counting every bill, paycheck and planned purchase you’ve added.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices and in your own iCloud account, which keeps your iPhone, iPad and Mac in sync. There are no accounts, no servers and no tracking. We can’t see your data.

I’m freelance. What about taxes?

Mark income as freelance and Future Cash sets aside a share for tax, so that money is never counted as spendable. You choose the percentage in Settings.
